# Third-Party Risk Management: Building a Risk-Tiered Vendor Program Under NIST CSF 2.0

A benefits administrator treats every vendor the same: one questionnaire, filed once, never revisited. This program redesigns vendor risk management under NIST CSF 2.0's supply chain risk category, with assessment depth, contract terms, and monitoring cadence scaled to how much risk each vendor actually carries.

The company and its vendors are built for this case study.

**Framework:** NIST CSF 2.0, Govern Function, GV.SC (Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management); NIST SP 1305 (Quick-Start Guide for Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management); NIST IR 8179 (Criticality Analysis Process Model)
**Tags:** NIST CSF, GRC Engineering, Third-Party Risk
