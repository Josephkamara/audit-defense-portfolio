# Projects

Four case studies. Each one is a realistic scenario, built to show the reasoning behind a decision, not just a completed checklist.

- [FedRAMP 20x Transition POA&M](fedramp-20x-poam/): closing the machine-readable evidence gap before FedRAMP 20x's Consolidated Rules take effect.
- [Risk Acceptance Memorandum](risk-acceptance-memo/): a TLS encryption gap on a legacy healthcare claims gateway.
- [SOC 2 Evidence Automation](soc2-access-review-automation/): a quarterly access review redesigned as a continuous control.
- [Third-Party Risk Management](third-party-risk-management/): a benefits administrator's vendor risk program, tiered under NIST CSF 2.0's supply chain risk category.
