# Projects

Eight case studies. Each one is a realistic scenario, built to show the reasoning behind a decision, not just a completed checklist.

- [FedRAMP 20x Transition POA&M](fedramp-20x-poam/): closing the machine-readable evidence gap before FedRAMP 20x's Consolidated Rules take effect.
- [Risk Acceptance Memorandum](risk-acceptance-memo/): a TLS encryption gap on a legacy healthcare claims gateway.
- [SOC 2 Evidence Automation](soc2-access-review-automation/): a quarterly access review redesigned as a continuous control.
- [Third-Party Risk Management](third-party-risk-management/): a benefits administrator's vendor risk program, tiered under NIST CSF 2.0's supply chain risk category.
- [ISO/IEC 27001 Statement of Applicability](iso27001-statement-of-applicability/): justifying which Annex A controls apply, and which do not, for a remote-first SaaS company.
- [PCI DSS Network Segmentation](pci-dss-network-segmentation/): defending which cardholder data environment boundaries actually hold up under testing.
- [EU AI Act High-Risk Classification](eu-ai-act-high-risk-classification/): why an employee monitoring feature's exemption claim fails once the system profiles the people it scores.
- [GRC Control Automation](grc-control-automation/): why a shared MFA control passed two audits and failed the third once someone checked the actual session logs.
